ME-1 Mate

ME-1 Mate is a free editor for the Allen & Heath ME-1 personal mixer. Set up the 16 keys of an ME-1 config (.ME1) and name the channels of an ME-U hub (.meu), in your browser. It works offline once loaded. Not affiliated with Allen & Heath.

How it works

  1. Pick a key. The ME-1 has 16 keys. Choose the one you want to set up.
  2. Pick its channels. Press channel numbers to give them to the key. A key can play one channel or a group.
  3. Set levels and export. Give the key a label, set its levels, then export the .ME1 and the .meu.
